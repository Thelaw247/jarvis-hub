# jv-command-bar (C05)

Ctrl+K or / opens it. Type to go to a view, a branch, his work (an open thread opens at its resume point: a PDF in the hub's reader at /read/<thread>; something due opens its thread or branch; a file its branch), a fact or a setting, or to ask Jarvis. Keyboard only works: arrows, Enter, Esc. At most five results plus "Ask Jarvis". `.open()`, `.close()`; exports `fuzzy`, `buildIndex`, `workIndex`, `search`.

Scene: `?scene=component:command-bar` (add `&state=<name>` for one state, `&theme=evening` for Evening). Details are in the comment at the top of `command-bar.js`.
