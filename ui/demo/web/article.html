The method of joints, step by step

Statics Notes · demo page, served by the hub

A truss is a set of two-force members joined at pins. Because each member only pushes or pulls along its own line, every joint is a particle in equilibrium: the forces on it add up to zero in x and in y.

The steps

  1. Find the support reactions from the whole truss (sum of moments about one support first).
  2. Pick a joint with at most two unknown member forces.
  3. Draw its free body. Assume every unknown member is in tension (pulling away from the joint).
  4. Solve ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0. A negative answer means compression.
  5. Move to the next joint, carrying the forces you found.

Check yourself

The last joint has no unknowns left: its equations should balance on their own. If they don't, an earlier sign is wrong.
