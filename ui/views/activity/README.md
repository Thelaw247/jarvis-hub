# Activity (V04)

Receipts. Waiting for you at the top (approval cards, plus the ones he asked to change, shown as "Changing…"), then what Jarvis did, newest first, with Undo and how long it still works ("Undo · until 9:12 am tomorrow"), his "no"s as quiet rows, filters by kind (hidden when there is nothing to filter), and Held back (what it chose not to interrupt with, and why). A brand-new day is one plain card with one action.

Focus never drops to the page: after Yes it lands on the new receipt, after No on the quiet row, after Change it on the "Changing…" card, after Undo on the undone row; otherwise the next card or row, or the h1. Saying yes anywhere shows a "Booked." toast with Undo.

Scenes: `activity`, `activity-clear`, `activity-empty`, `activity-changing`. Add `&theme=evening` or `&size=laptop|phone|ambient`. Details are in the comments at the top of each file. Keyboard tests: `tests/e2e/test_activity_keyboard.py`.
