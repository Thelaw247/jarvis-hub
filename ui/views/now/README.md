# Now (V01)

The hub's home page, as empty and calm as Huw's (HUD v3, `docs/design/JARVIS_DESIGN_HUD_V2.md` §14).

**The calm home** (the default; Controls › Look › "Where Jarvis shows things" = Stage window): the centre drawn by `ambient.js`. Awake, the orb alone in the middle of the grid (white rings). Resting (two idle minutes, or nobody here), the small standby sphere with a small mono clock and a tiny tracked date under it. Nothing else: no clock while awake, no date, no "what's next" line, no day dial (what's next is in Today and on the Today icon's badge). The orb keeps one box and one centre in both states, sized so its outer ornaments are about a sixth of the screen's width. The shell adds the icon row (`jv-rail`, with search and the page dots), Jarvis's caption above it while he speaks, and the status word bottom-left. The windows Jarvis pulls up go to the **Stage** (a separate window); while any are up, the icon row shows a small Stage glyph with their count (this view sets `rail.stage` and answers its `jv-rail-stage` event with `showStage()`). The corner orb steps aside (`data-home="calm"`): one orb.

**The return offer** (`offer.js`; HUD v2 §6.4, §9.5, §10): the home never carries a permanent "what you were doing" line. When Jarvis has an offer (a piece of work left between two minutes and three hours ago, not in the morning before any work, never with others present or from a tier-2 branch), it is his caption above the icon row while he says it, and otherwise **one small glass card under the orb** (under the small clock when resting): the page's picture, "Carry on" and "PPS 7 · Q7 · Today", plus a quiet "Not now". A tap is his yes: a PDF opens in the reader already at the question, with no second card (`accepted()` in the reader). "Not now", 45 s with no answer, opening an icon or going to another view all take the card away without a word; the thread stays on the Homework icon (which opens a PDF thread in the reader too). It is offered again only when something changes: he leaves and comes back (the screen wakes), or the spot moves. Answers live in `store.ui.offers` for the page load.

**On the hub** (the old behaviour, and always on the phone, which has no Stage): resting shows the same standby centre; awake, the window canvas (`ui/views/stage/canvas.js`) holds at most six windows counting the now card, on a 12-column grid sized to the screen so it never scrolls. Waking, the orb slides into the corner over the canvas (`slideOrbToDock()`); nothing is dropped (windows that step aside dock into their icon on the row).

`widgets.js` turns a pushed window into its content from data the hub already has (used by the canvas on both screens).

Scenes: `now-calm` (awake, nothing up: Huw's awake screen), `now-offer`, `now-offer-spoken`, `now-ambient-resume` (the return offer), `now` (Jarvis speaking, four windows on the Stage), `now-calm-waiting`, `now-ambient`, `now-ambient-waiting`, `now-ambient-listening`, `now-ambient-others`, `now-ambient-midnight`, `now-ambient-caption`, `now-ambient-rail`, `now-offline`, and On the hub: `now-awake`, `now-active`, `now-waiting`, `now-full`, `now-crowded`, `now-rail`, `now-marks`, `now-wants`, `now-focus`, `now-rail-open`.
